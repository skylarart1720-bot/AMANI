Find your way in.

AMANI brings support information, referral contacts and requests for human help into one place.

Visitors

No registration is needed. Ask a question, find an organisation or check a suspicious link.

Moderators

Authorised staff use a separate dashboard to respond to requests and maintain information.

  1. Open amani-navy.vercel.app on your phone or computer.
  2. Choose EN or FR. On a phone, open the menu to see the navigation.
  3. Choose Get support, type a short question and press the send arrow. AI replies are optional.
  4. Need a person? Select Request human support and keep the same browser tab open.

Know what AMANI offers

AMANI provides information and referrals. It does not replace a lawyer, clinician or emergency service. A human-support request does not guarantee an immediate response.

VISITOR / GET SUPPORT
VISITOR / GET SUPPORT

Start a conversation.

You can begin with a simple sentence. You do not need to share your name, phone number or address.

  1. Choose Get support. Click a starter suggestion, or type directly into Your message. Suggestions fill the box; you still need to send.
  2. Use the Topic selector if you know the area you need. Otherwise leave Let Amani help me find it selected.
  3. Decide whether to tick Use AI replies. Ticking it permits recent conversation text to be sent to the configured AI provider.
  4. Press the send arrow or Enter. Wait for the response before sending again. Each message can contain up to 4,000 characters.
  5. Read the reply and any suggested contacts under Direct support. Ask a follow-up question in the same box.
Try this fictional example

“I would like information about staying safe online.”

Understand the type of reply

Reply or labelWhat it means
Amani / AI responseAn automated reply. AI needs your consent and a working provider. Check important details against the linked sources.
Directory responseA reply based on topic and referral information. It can be used without AI consent or when AI is unavailable.
Human moderatorA message sent by an authorised person after a human-support request.

Change your AI choice

Untick Use AI replies before your next message to stop requesting AI replies. This does not recall text already sent to a provider. The checkbox may be disabled if AI is not configured.

Language and continuity

English and French interfaces and automated support are supported. Source material and moderator messages remain in their original language. Keep the same tab open: there is no account-based conversation recovery.

What staff can see

Referring a conversation to the queue lets authorised moderators read that conversation. Share only what is needed. Deleting the conversation also removes its linked support request from this service.

VISITOR / FIND HELP
VISITOR / FIND HELP

Find an organisation.

The referral directory helps you move from a general question to an official contact channel.

  1. Choose Find help, or select Open support directory from the chat screen.
  2. Type a topic or organisation into Search organisations. Results update as you type. Try “Cyber” as a simple example.
  3. Use Coverage area to choose Ghana, International or All coverage areas.
  4. Read the service description, coverage, hours and contact-check note on the card.
  5. Select the phone number, when provided, to open your device’s calling app. Choose Official website to visit the organisation in a new tab.
Contact noteWhat to do
Contact checked [date]Use the date as context; still confirm current availability directly.
Contact details need confirmationCheck the organisation’s own channels before relying on its details.
No results?

Clear the search, try a broader word and reset the coverage filter. Calls or external websites may have their own charges, access requirements and privacy terms.

VISITOR / CHECK A LINK
VISITOR / CHECK A LINK

Pause before opening a link.

A reputation check looks for known threats. It cannot promise that a website is safe.

  1. Choose Check a link. Copy the address without visiting it and paste the full https:// or http:// address into Website URL.
  2. Do not submit private invitation, password-reset or sign-in links. Remove personal information and secret tokens before sharing a URL.
  3. Read and tick the consent box. The URL may be shared with reputation providers for lookup or fresh scanning; reports may be public.
  4. Select Check link. Wait while AMANI checks the providers. A fresh analysis can take around two minutes and may still remain unverified.
  5. Read the result, reasons, provider statuses and check time. The check does not require you to open the submitted link.
ResultWhat it means / what to do
Threat reportedA provider reported a threat. Avoid opening the link or entering information.
No known threats reportedNo current detection was returned. This is not a safety guarantee.
Fresh analysis in progressA provider is still analysing the URL. Keep the page open and wait for the update.
Unable to verify this linkThere is no reliable current verdict. The provider may be unavailable, unconfigured or have insufficient results. Treat the link as unverified.

Report a cyber incident

The side panel offers Call 292 and an Official reporting page for Ghana’s Cyber Security Authority. A link check does not file a report for you.

Official reporting information: csa.gov.gh/report.

Consent is required

The Check link button stays disabled until you enter an address and tick the sharing consent. If no live providers are configured, the interface says that results will be unknown.

VISITOR / PRIVACY & SAFETY
VISITOR / PRIVACY & SAFETY

Stay in control of your conversation.

Open Privacy & your data in the navigation, or Privacy & safeguarding in the footer, to read the in-app explanation.

Delete a conversation

  1. Select Delete conversation in the navigation or the bin icon in the chat header.
  2. Read the confirmation. Choose Keep conversation to cancel, or Delete conversation to continue.
  3. Wait for the message confirming deletion. Messages and linked support requests are removed from this service; the action cannot be undone.

Leave quickly

Select Quick exit at the top of the page. AMANI attempts to delete the current conversation and immediately sends the tab to Google. It does not ask for confirmation. Network failure can prevent the deletion.

Privacy featureWhat it does—and its limit
No visitor accountNo identity is required. A random access token is held in the browser tab; it is not an account you can sign back into.
Encrypted storageThe support workflow encrypts stored messages. This is not a claim of end-to-end encryption: the service processes messages and referred chats are visible to moderators.
Seven-day retentionThe support workflow expires conversations after seven days. Do not rely on AMANI as a permanent record.
Deletion and quick exitNeither clears browser/network history, backups or records held by external providers. AI consent and URL-sharing consent are separate choices.
Emergency help

AMANI is not an emergency service. The Ghana emergency strip offers Call 112; it opens a call action on a compatible device. Outside Ghana, use your local emergency number. Do not wait for a moderator in immediate danger.

Ghana emergency contact: National Ambulance Service. Close a privacy dialog with its × button or by clicking outside it.

MODERATOR / ACCESS

Open the moderator workspace.

This separate site is for authorised support staff. Visitors and funders do not need a moderator token to use the public website.

MODERATOR DASHBOARDamani-hck2.vercel.app ↗
  1. Obtain the current moderator access token privately from the system administrator.
  2. Open the dashboard link above. Paste the token into Access token, then choose Sign in. No username or email is currently requested.
  3. After sign-in, use Support queue, Referral directory or Knowledge review.
  4. Check Last refreshed. Choose Refresh to reload information manually. The workspace also updates automatically.
  5. Select Sign out when finished, especially on a shared computer.
How access works

The dashboard validates the token against the backend and uses a one-hour sign-in cookie. If you are returned to sign-in, enter the current token again. Ask the administrator if it is rejected; do not send it in screenshots or public messages.

The current access model uses a shared staff token. Individual staff accounts, MFA and per-person access auditing are not part of this interface.

MODERATOR / SUPPORT QUEUE
MODERATOR / SUPPORT QUEUE

Read, reply and update a case.

Work from the visitor’s existing conversation so they receive the reply in the same place.

  1. Open Support queue. The counters show Queued, Critical and In progress cases. Critical counts exclude resolved cases.
  2. Use Case status to filter All cases, Queued, In progress or Resolved. Select a case card to see its reference, priority, status, time and messages.
  3. Read the conversation. Use the status selector beside the case reference to mark it In progress while handling it.
  4. Write in Reply to visitor and choose Send reply. Replies can contain up to 4,000 characters. The visitor sees a Human moderator message.
  5. Continue the conversation, then choose Resolved when appropriate under your team’s process. Use the selector to change status again if further work is needed.
Priority supports judgement

Critical is an automated routing flag, not a clinical assessment. Read the content and follow your team’s escalation process; do not assume every urgent case will be flagged.

If a case disappears, the visitor may have deleted the chat or the session may have expired. The dashboard has no account-based recovery or case-assignment control. Coordinate responsibilities within your team.

MODERATOR / REFERRAL DIRECTORY
MODERATOR / REFERRAL DIRECTORY

Keep contact information useful.

Saved contacts appear in the public directory. Verify the details before making a change.

  1. Open Referral directory. Choose Edit beside a contact, or Add organisation for a new one.
  2. Fill in the fields below. For an existing contact, keep its id unchanged; it identifies the record.
  3. Choose the Category and write a clear Service description. Use plain language about what the organisation actually offers.
  4. Select Save contact. Look for Directory updated. Choose Cancel if you do not want to save.
  5. Check the public Find help page after refreshing it. Confirm the result reads correctly and links to the intended organisation.
FieldWhat to enter
idA unique short identifier for a new record, e.g. example-support. Use lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only (up to 60 characters).
title / organisationThe support listing’s title and the organisation’s name.
regionUse Ghana or International to match the public coverage filters.
phone / websiteAn optional phone number and the official HTTPS website.
hoursVerified service hours, or a clear instruction to confirm availability.
verified atThe date you actually checked the contact. Leave it blank if not confirmed.
Category / Service descriptionThe relevant support topic (or emergency) and a factual description of the service.
Saving is a public change

There is no separate approval step for directory edits. This screen does not provide a delete-contact action or a language-list editor. Do not represent a listing as an AMANI partnership unless that is established.

MODERATOR / KNOWLEDGE REVIEW
MODERATOR / KNOWLEDGE REVIEW

Review information before it is used.

Knowledge review controls which source material is available to the automated assistant.

  1. Open Knowledge review. Under Submit source material, enter the title, source/publisher, HTTPS source URL and date checked.
  2. Choose the Topic. Add the checked material in Reviewed source text (20–6,000 characters). This form accepts text and a link, not a file upload.
  3. Select Submit for review. The new entry is pending and is not yet available to the assistant.
  4. Under Editorial review, read the entry and open its source link. Check relevance, accuracy and the verification date before deciding.
  5. Choose Approve & publish to make it available for future relevant replies, or Reject to keep it unavailable.
  6. For published material that should no longer be used, choose Unpublish. Withdrawal affects future retrieval; it does not erase past replies.
What the status means

Pending: awaiting a decision. Published: eligible for use by the assistant. Rejected: not used, including entries withdrawn with Unpublish. An unpublished/rejected entry can be approved again.

Publishing is an editorial action, not proof of legal or clinical accuracy. The screen does not offer an edit or delete action for existing entries. For a correction, withdraw the old entry and submit reviewed replacement text.

OPTIONAL CHANNEL / WHATSAPP
OPTIONAL CHANNEL / WHATSAPP

Use WhatsApp when it is activated.

This channel requires a configured WhatsApp Business number and Meta integration. Do not assume that deploying the website has activated it.

  1. Obtain the official AMANI WhatsApp number from the AMANI team. This guide does not supply a number because activation has not been confirmed.
  2. Send a text message to start. Read the welcome message and its privacy explanation.
  3. Continue with a support question or use one of the exact text commands below.
Send this textWhat it does
enable aiConsents to sharing recent messages with the AI provider for AI replies.
disable aiStops requesting AI replies. Directory-based support remains available.
humanCreates a request for a moderator.
updatesRetrieves the latest available human replies (up to the last three). Use it to check whether a moderator has answered.
forgetDeletes the linked conversation from AMANI. It does not delete messages from WhatsApp, Meta or your device.
Replies and session expiry

Proactive WhatsApp delivery of human replies is not implemented: send updates to retrieve them. If told that the session expired, send your message again to start a new conversation.

What is different from the website?

Meta can see the WhatsApp phone number. AI is opt-in, and AMANI conversations expire after seven days. The web and WhatsApp channels do not provide an account-based way to merge or recover conversations.

This is a text support channel. This guide does not promise voice calls, voice-note support, attachments, proactive template messages or the website’s link-check form inside WhatsApp.

HELP / TROUBLESHOOTING

If something does not work.

Start with the message on screen. Avoid sending the same request repeatedly.

What you seeWhat to do next
Connecting to support or service unavailableCheck your internet connection, wait briefly and refresh the same tab. If it persists, tell the AMANI team the page and time. Do not include confidential messages.
AI is unavailable or a directory response appearsYou can still use directory support. If the AI choice is enabled, tick consent before sending. Provider availability or credits may still prevent AI replies.
Send arrow is disabledEnter some text and wait for the current request to finish. Keep the message within 4,000 characters.
No moderator responseKeep the same tab open. Check the case status. Queued requests do not guarantee a response time or that someone is currently online.
Conversation has disappearedDeletion, expiry, cleared browser storage or losing the original tab can remove access. Start a new chat; a case reference alone cannot restore it.
No directory matchesClear the search and choose All coverage areas. Try a broader topic or organisation name.
Link check stays pending or unknownWait for the scan update. If it remains unknown, leave the link unverified; do not treat missing detections as proof of safety.
Moderator token is rejectedUse the current hosted-system token, with no quotes or extra spaces. Ask your administrator if it changed. A local development token may not work online.
Dashboard returns to sign-inYour sign-in cookie may have expired, or the token may have changed. Sign in again.
Save or submit failsCheck required fields, HTTPS source links, text lengths, the date and the contact id format. Correct the issue and retry once.
Updates look staleUse the dashboard’s Refresh button. On the public site, check the connection and refresh the same tab if necessary.
Ask for help without exposing data

Share the feature name, an approximate time and the error wording with the AMANI team. Remove access tokens, names, contact details and private conversation content from screenshots.

Prepare before presenting

Test the hosted flows, confirm provider availability and arrange a moderator. Use a separate demonstration environment for changing contacts or publishing example material. Do not publish fictional records into the live support service.

Describe the system accurately

The interface connects visitors to information, referral channels and a human-support queue. Provider availability, trained staffing and verified contacts determine what the service can deliver at any moment.

Do not present a working interface as evidence of guaranteed emergency response, confirmed partnerships, clinical validation or measured impact. Bring verified usage and outcome figures separately if discussing results.
